Six layers of quality

Growth 25 · Unit economics 20 · Survival 20 · Management 15 · Catalysts 15 · Valuation 5

Every model indicator

All model inputs are listed below.

Growth & market

  • Revenue growth (%)
  • Growth acceleration (pp)
  • Market share (%)

Unit economics

  • Gross margin (%)
  • Margin change (pp)
  • Operating leverage

Survival & dilution

  • Positive free cash flow
  • Cash runway (months)
  • Share-count growth (%)
  • Verified share buyback
  • Debt level

Management & ownership

  • Insider ownership (%)
  • Founder-led management
  • Insider share purchases
  • Institutional ownership (%)

Catalysts & trends

  • Megatrend alignment
  • Growth catalyst
  • Competitive moat

Valuation

  • EV / sales

Limits and contextual indicators

  • Market cap (million USD)
  • Daily traded value (thousand USD)
  • Annual market size (million USD)
  • Two-year price multiple
  • Tradable on a German exchange

Seven risk flags

  • Going-concern uncertainty
  • Repeated dilution
  • Reverse stock split
  • Customer concentration
  • Auditor change
  • OTC / shell company
  • Non-recurring revenue

Evidence sufficiency

Unknown numeric scoring inputs earn no points; at least three counted unknowns downgrade a qualifying result to review.
