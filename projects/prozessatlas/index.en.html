Strategy room

From situation to decision, in four steps

The tools are a process themselves: first clarify the direction, then link goals, then weight criteria, then evaluate options. The weights from step 3 can be applied directly in step 4.

Source note. The process structure (categories and level-2 process groups) is based on the APQC Process Classification Framework® (PCF), Cross-Industry, version 7.x, adapted for this presentation.

APQC’s Process Classification Framework® (PCF) is an open standard developed by APQC, a nonprofit that promotes benchmarking and best practices worldwide. The PCF is intended to facilitate organizational improvement through process management and benchmarking, regardless of industry, size, or geography. To download the full PCF or industry-specific versions of the PCF, as well as associated measures and benchmarking, please visit www.apqc.org/pcf.

KPIs, target values, weights, roles and all measurements are demo data of a fictional company. The maturity model is an original development based on the PDCA cycle. © KeyOne · Keywan Fakhri
